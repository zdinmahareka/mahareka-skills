# Routing — which desk owns the work

Rooms and desks follow Mahareka's structure. Route by the work, not by who asked.

| Room | Desk | Send here |
|---|---|---|
| Sales & Marketing | Generate Lead | content, captions, ads, posters, campaigns, referral and networking messages |
| Sales & Marketing | Convert Lead to Customer | lead qualification (Rule of 5), follow-ups, objections, proposals, closing, design-fee explanation |
| Fulfillment | Commercial & QS | rates, costing, BQ, quotation, BQ audit, subcon quotes, VE, budget options, cost control, claims value |
| Fulfillment | Design Dept | client brief, measurement, design phases, presentations, revisions, design approval, drawings |
| Fulfillment | Project Management & Supervision | programme, lookahead, site diary, progress, safety, inspections, defects, handover, client progress updates |
| Business Support | Admin & HR | people, records, work orders, office administration |
| Business Support | Finance & Account | payments, invoices, collections, project expenses, cashflow |
| Business Support | Legal | contract terms, payment terms, variation and warranty wording, compliance |
| Control room | Chief of Staff | routing, daily brief, what is stuck, anything spanning several desks |

## Handover order (work flows this way)

Enquiry → Generate Lead → Convert Lead → **handover** → Design + QS (design, costing, approval)
→ Project Management (preparation, execution, pre-handover, handover) → handed over.
Business Support serves every desk.

A handover is complete only when the receiving desk accepts it. Design approval and price
acceptance are separate records. Design payment does not authorise construction.

## Work that spans desks

Split it: say which desk does which part and in what order. Do the first part in that role; list
the rest as next actions with their desk.
